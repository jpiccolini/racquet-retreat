How to use this page

  1. Browse the Tennis, Pickleball and Someday tabs under "The options."
  2. Tap 2, 4 or 6 to see prices for that size group, and sort by Most hours or Lowest cost.
  3. Tap Add to my picks on up to five places you like. Open What we checked on a card for details and sources.
  4. Scroll to Your ballot: choose your name, put your picks in order, rate each group size, and add your play level, budget and the weekends that work.
  5. Tap Save my ballot. It shows up on the group board right away, and you can change it any time. Add ideas on the Idea board and comments on any resort card.
